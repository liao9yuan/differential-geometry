# H2PrincipalPair

`appD2_pair_h2` is the reusable rank-two principal-form estimate.  A
pointwise bound for a rank-four coefficient, together with the finite
rank-two curvature-action packet, controls its action against
`(1 - Δ_∇) W` by the square of the spectral `H²` norm of `W`.
`appD2_pair_h4` is its exact order-four energy face, obtained by taking
`W = (1 - Δ_∇) U`; it introduces no new regularity hypothesis.

The proof deliberately stays at pairing level.  It uses only the finite `H²`
Bochner comparison and does not convert an `H⁴` norm into four covariant
derivatives.  Focused verification passed under the Route-(c) resource cap.
