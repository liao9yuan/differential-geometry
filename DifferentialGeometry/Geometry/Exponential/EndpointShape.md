# EndpointShape

## Status

`intrJacobi_smul` is now a public exponential-layer identity and passed focused, targeted, and full-project verification. The duplicate private proof formerly in `RadialLaplacian.lean` was removed.

The identity states that simultaneously scaling a radial launch vector and its Jacobi launch direction is equivalent to scaling time. Together with `covDeriv_comp_mul`, it supplies the legal all-time field and covariant-derivative reparameterization needed by endpoint comparison; the endpoint-only `jacobiVar_smul` is not sufficient for that use.
