# UnifTopPathPair

`top_pair_h2_unif` specializes the generic principal-form estimate to the
integrated top-path deviation.  Its radius and constant are selected before
the metric varies over the order-three class.  Only the pointwise half of
`top_path_dev_unif` and the rank-two finite `H²` curvature-action comparison
are used; no fourth metric jet or prior `H⁴` state radius enters.
`top_pair_h4_unif` is the exact Rung-3 principal energy specialization; its
`H⁴` norm occurs only on the right-hand side as the absorbable energy.
`top_pair_abs_unif` then chooses an `H²` cap before the metric varies so that
twice the absolute principal form is bounded by any prescribed
`η · ‖U‖²_{H⁴}`.

This theorem controls only the principal form.  It does not yet bound the
commutator and lower pieces of the full diagonal Rung-3 pairing.  Focused
verification passed under the Route-(c) resource cap.
